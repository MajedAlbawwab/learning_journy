#  On-Premise Networks

Definition: An on-premises network is a network whose infrastructure is hosted at the organization's own physical location, such as its office or data center.

Key characteristics:

- The organization manages its own network infrastructure.
    
- Uses physical devices such as routers, switches, firewalls, and servers.
    
- Provides direct control over hardware and network configuration.
    
- Requires maintenance, space, power, and hardware costs.
    

Example: A company has its own servers and Cisco switches inside its office building.
