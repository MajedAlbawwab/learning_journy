# What is a Network? || Network Types

-also called (computer network) is 2 or more devices need to share information between them
- to do that you need a common media between them to share this information

local area network(LAN)
- users in the same room/department connected with a switch device
- or users in different rooms/departments connected with router and some switches

Wide Area Network(WAN)
- users connected globally through the internet
- a service
