> [!summary]
> A network (also called a computer network) is two or more devices that share information through a common medium. A LAN connects users in the same room or department, while a WAN connects users globally through the internet and needs a service provider and a group of devices.

## What is a Network?

- A <span style="color:#e07b39">network</span> (also called a <span style="color:#e07b39">computer network</span>) is 2 or more devices that need to share information between them
- To do that, you need a <span style="color:#3b82f6">common medium</span> between them to share this information

## Network Types

### Local Area Network (LAN)

- A <span style="color:#e07b39">LAN</span> connects users in a small area
- <span style="color:#22c55e">Example 1:</span> users in the same room/department connected with a <span style="color:#3b82f6">switch</span>
- <span style="color:#22c55e">Example 2:</span> users in different rooms/departments connected with a <span style="color:#3b82f6">router</span> and some switches

### Wide Area Network (WAN)

- A <span style="color:#e07b39">WAN</span> connects users globally through the internet
- A <span style="color:#3b82f6">service provider</span> is needed
- A group of devices (<span style="color:#3b82f6">routers</span>, <span style="color:#3b82f6">switches</span>, etc.) is needed

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Network (computer network) | 2 or more devices that need to share information between them | — |
| Common medium | The shared connection between devices that lets them exchange information | — |
| LAN (Local Area Network) | Users in the same room/department connected with a switch, or in different rooms/departments connected with a router and some switches | <span style="color:#22c55e">Users in the same room connected with a switch</span> |
| WAN (Wide Area Network) | Users connected globally through the internet, requiring a service provider and a group of devices | <span style="color:#22c55e">Users connected globally through the internet</span> |
