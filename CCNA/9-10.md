1-  What is a Network? || Network Types
what is network ? :

also called(computer network) its 2 or more devices needs to share information between them

 -to do that they will need a common media between them to share than information

network types:

Local Area Network(LAN):

-users in the same room/department connected using switches device

-or some users from different rooms/departments connected using router and some switches

Wide Area Network(WAN):

users connected globally through the internet

-service providers will be needed

- a group of devices(routers-switches & other devices) will be needed
