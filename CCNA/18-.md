  - 4 pairs of copper

  - 2 pairs for 200mbps
  - 4 pairs for 1000mbps
  - shielded(STP) and unshielded(UTP)

 B- optical fibers , new , already in high speeds

 - starts with 1 gbps , up to tens of gbps
 - either light or laser

 2 types of transmission media are used light or laser :
 multimedia(MM) : light for short distance
 single mode(SM) : laser for long distance

## SFP (Small Form-Factor Pluggable)

Definition: An SFP is a removable transceiver module used to connect a switch or router to a network through fiber-optic or copper cabling.

The module converts signals between the device's electrical interface and the transmission medium—for example, electrical signals to optical signals for fiber
